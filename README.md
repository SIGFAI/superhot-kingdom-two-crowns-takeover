# Kingdom Two Crowns Takeover

**superhot** × Kingdom Two Crowns · visual · idea by the team

Bring Kingdom Two Crowns into the game in the most visible and funny way. The change shows in the first 30 seconds of play.

> I turned SUPERHOT into Kingdom Two Crowns: crowned Greed, archers whose arrows hang in frozen time, coins everywhere, and a monarch charge.

## Install

Install BepInEx 5 (64-bit) into your SUPERHOT folder, then copy the mod's BepInEx/plugins/SigfMix folder into it and start the game. You land in a Kingdom camp: stand still to freeze time, move to let arrows, coins and Greed fly, and collect two crowns to call the monarch.
